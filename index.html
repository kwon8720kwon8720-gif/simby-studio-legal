Creator publishing tool

Publish your gameplay clips without leaving your desk.

Simby Studio takes the short videos you already record, lets you set the title, privacy, and interaction settings, and publishes them to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts through each platform's official API. Nothing is uploaded until you review a preview and give explicit consent.

What it does

◆

Official API publishing

Uploads run through the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API. No automation of the mobile app, no scraping, no unofficial endpoints.

◈

You choose every setting

Privacy level has no preselected default. Comment, Duet, and Stitch stay off until you turn them on. Commercial disclosure is off by default.

◉

Preview before consent

You see the exact clip, title, and settings that will be published, then tick an explicit consent box. Upload status is reported back to you until it completes.

Built for one job, done properly

Your account, your content

Simby Studio only ever posts to accounts you personally authorize through the platform's own OAuth screen. It is not a bulk cross-posting service and it does not redistribute other people's videos.

Revoke access any time

Access can be withdrawn from your TikTok or Google account security settings at any moment. Once revoked, Simby Studio can no longer read your profile or publish on your behalf.

Questions about data handling? The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to request deletion. The Terms of Service covers acceptable use.
