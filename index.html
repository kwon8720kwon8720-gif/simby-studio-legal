What Simby Studio is

Simby Studio (심비 스튜디오) is a public creator product. It publishes your own short gameplay videos to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is not a private experiment, not a bulk cross-poster, and not a scraper of other people’s content.

Your account only

Publishing uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. You approve permissions on TikTok’s own screen. Simby Studio never sees your password.

You decide every setting

Privacy has no preselected value. Comment, Duet, and Stitch stay off until you turn them on. Commercial disclosure starts off.

Preview, then consent

You see the clip, title, and settings first. The Post button stays locked until you tick express consent in the same session.

TikTok permissions we request

Only the scopes required to show the destination account and to publish the file you selected.

ScopeWhy it is needed in this product
user.info.basic Show which TikTok account will receive the post, and read that account’s posting limits such as maximum video duration.
video.upload Transfer the video you selected so it can be previewed before publishing.
video.publish Complete the publish after you have chosen settings and given express in-app consent.

How to test this page in one minute: pick a short MP4 → choose a privacy status (nothing is preselected) → leave Comment/Duet/Stitch off unless you want them → keep commercial disclosure off unless it applies → tick express consent → press Post. The package box lists the exact choices from this session.
