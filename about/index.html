About Simby Studio

Simby Studio — also written 심비 스튜디오 in Korean — is a shipped creator product. The website you are on is the live publisher, not a holding page.

What we build

Game creators already record short clips at their desk. Simby Studio is the last step: review the clip, choose privacy and interaction settings, disclose commercial content if it applies, and publish to the creator’s own TikTok or YouTube account through each platform’s official API.

The product is public. It is made for creators, not for a closed private test group. Anyone who owns the footage and the destination account can use it under the Terms of Service.

What we refuse to build

  • Posting to accounts the user did not personally authorize.
  • Scraping, mirroring, or re-uploading other creators’ videos.
  • Bulk or scheduled posting farms.
  • Selling, sharing, or advertising against user data.
  • Watermarks, logos, or branding stamped onto the user’s file.

How TikTok is used

Login Kit authenticates the creator on TikTok’s own screen. user.info.basic identifies the destination account. video.upload and video.publish move the selected file after express consent in the publisher. Full scope rationale is on Features. Data handling is on Security and in the Privacy Policy.
